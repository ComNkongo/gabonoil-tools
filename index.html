Buoyancy Can

The offshore buoyancy module is a piece of equipment designed to provide controlled positive lift to underwater installations. It is used to reduce the apparent weight of submerged equipment and facilitate their handling, installation, and stabilization in offshore environments. Offshore applications Installation and stabilization of risers Support for subsea lines Subsea lifting and positioning operations Reduction of loads on offshore structures Key advantages
